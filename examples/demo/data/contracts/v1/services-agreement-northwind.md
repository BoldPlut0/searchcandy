# Master Services Agreement

Agreement number: MSA-2026-014
Between: Brightline Analytics, Inc. ("Brightline")
And: Northwind Health, Inc. ("Customer")
Effective date: March 3, 2026

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") sets out the terms on which Brightline provides its analytics platform and related services to Customer.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Platform" means Brightline's hosted analytics software, including any updates Brightline makes generally available.

1.2 "Customer Data" means data that Customer or its users upload to the Platform.

1.3 "Order Form" means an ordering document signed by both parties that refers to this Agreement.

1.4 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in New York.

## 2. Services

2.1 Brightline will provide the Platform and the support services described in Schedule A for the term of each Order Form.

2.2 Brightline will make the Platform available 99.5% of each calendar month, not counting scheduled maintenance announced at least five Business Days in advance.

2.3 Customer may add users at any time. Added users are charged pro rata for the rest of the current billing period.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Customer will pay the fees set out in each Order Form.

3.2 Invoices are issued monthly and are payable within thirty days of receipt.

3.3 Late amounts accrue interest at 1% per month or the highest rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.4 Fees do not include taxes. Customer is responsible for sales, use and similar taxes, other than taxes on Brightline's income.

3.5 Brightline may raise its fees once a year, by no more than 5%, with at least sixty days' written notice.

## 4. Term and Renewal

4.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until every Order Form has ended.

4.2 Each Order Form has an initial term of twenty-four months. It renews automatically for further twelve-month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety days before the end of the current term.

## 5. Termination

5.1 Either party may terminate this agreement with thirty days' written notice.

5.2 Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within fifteen days of receiving notice of it.

5.3 On termination, Customer will pay all fees for services provided up to the termination date. Prepaid fees for any period after the termination date are refunded pro rata.

5.4 Sections 3, 6, 7, 8 and 10 survive termination.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party will keep the other party's confidential information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement.

6.2 These obligations last for three years after termination, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 7. Data Protection and Security

7.1 Brightline processes Customer Data only on Customer's documented instructions and in line with the Data Processing Addendum in Schedule B.

7.2 Brightline will tell Customer about any security incident affecting Customer Data without undue delay, and in any case within seventy-two hours of becoming aware of it.

7.3 Within ninety days after termination, Brightline will delete Customer Data from its systems, except copies it must keep by law.

7.4 Customer may export Customer Data at any time before termination using the Platform's export tools.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits, even if it was told such damages were possible.

8.2 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by Customer in the twelve months before the event that gave rise to the claim.

8.3 The limits in this Section 8 do not apply to a party's indemnity obligations, to a breach of confidentiality, or to gross negligence.

## 9. Indemnities

9.1 Brightline will defend Customer against any third-party claim that the Platform infringes that third party's intellectual property rights, and will pay any damages finally awarded on that claim.

9.2 Customer will defend Brightline against any third-party claim arising from Customer Data, or from Customer's use of the Platform in breach of this Agreement.

## 10. General

10.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by email to legal@brightline.example and legal@northwind-health.example, or by courier to each party's address on the Order Form.

10.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

10.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

10.4 This Agreement, its Schedules and the Order Forms are the entire agreement between the parties about their subject matter.

10.5 If any part of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the rest of it stays in force, and the unenforceable part is read as closely as possible to the parties' original intent.

## 11. Insurance

11.1 During the term of this Agreement, Brightline will keep commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 in the aggregate.

11.2 Brightline will send Customer a certificate of insurance within ten Business Days of a written request.

## 12. Subcontractors and Personnel

12.1 Brightline may use subcontractors to provide the services, and remains responsible for their work as if it were its own.

12.2 Brightline personnel with access to Customer Data must pass a background check and complete security training every year.

## 13. Publicity

13.1 Neither party may use the other party's name or logo in marketing material without the other party's prior written consent.

13.2 Brightline may list Customer as a customer in confidential materials it shares with its own investors and auditors.

## 14. Force Majeure

14.1 Neither party is liable for a delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as natural disasters, war, or the failure of a public utility, other than a failure to pay fees.

14.2 If such an event lasts more than thirty days, either party may end the affected Order Form by written notice.

## 15. Dispute Resolution

15.1 Before starting any court proceedings, the parties will try to settle a dispute through good-faith discussions between their senior executives for at least thirty days.

15.2 Either party may seek urgent relief from a court at any time to protect its confidential information or intellectual property.

## 16. Changes to the Platform

16.1 Brightline may improve the Platform from time to time, but will not make a change that materially reduces the functionality Customer has paid for during the current term.

16.2 Brightline will announce any feature it plans to retire at least six months before retiring it, and will help Customer move to a replacement feature at no extra cost.

## 17. Records and Audit

17.1 Each party will keep accurate records of the fees invoiced and paid under this Agreement for at least six years.

17.2 Customer may ask for a copy of the records that support any invoice, and Brightline will provide them within fifteen Business Days.

## Schedule A: Support Services

A.1 Support requests may be submitted by email on business days.

A.2 Brightline will respond to a Severity 1 request (the Platform is unavailable) within one hour, to a Severity 2 request (a major feature is not working) within four business hours, and to any other request within two Business Days.

A.3 Customer will name up to three support contacts who may open Severity 1 requests by phone.

A.4 Brightline will hold a service review with Customer once a quarter, covering availability, open support requests and planned changes.

## Schedule B: Data Processing Addendum (summary)

B.1 Brightline stores Customer Data in data centers in the United States.

B.2 Brightline may use the subprocessors listed on its website, and will give Customer thirty days' notice before adding a new one.

B.3 Customer may audit Brightline's compliance with this Addendum once a year, at Customer's cost, with thirty days' notice.

B.4 Brightline encrypts Customer Data in transit and at rest, and keeps backups for thirty-five days.

## Schedule C: Service Credits

C.1 If availability in a calendar month falls below 99.5%, Customer receives a credit of 5% of that month's fees.

C.2 If availability falls below 99.0%, the credit is 10% of that month's fees, and below 95.0% it is 25%.

C.3 Customer must claim a credit in writing within thirty days after the end of the month. Credits are Customer's only remedy for missed availability, except where availability falls below 95.0% in three consecutive months, in which case Customer may terminate the affected Order Form immediately.

## Schedule D: Order Form 1 (summary)

D.1 Platform subscription for up to 400 users: $1,040,000 a year.

D.2 Premium support, including a named support engineer: $200,000 a year.

D.3 Total annual fees: $1,240,000, invoiced in twelve equal monthly installments.
