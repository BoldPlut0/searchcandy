# Master Services Agreement

Agreement number: MSA-2025-031
Between: Brightline Analytics, Inc. ("Brightline")
And: Contoso Pharmacy, LLC ("Customer")
Effective date: September 15, 2025

## 1. Services

1.1 Brightline will provide its hosted analytics platform and standard support to Customer for the term of each Order Form.

1.2 Standard support is available by email on business days. Brightline will respond to urgent requests within four business hours.

## 2. Fees and Payment

2.1 Customer will pay the fees set out in each Order Form.

2.2 Invoices are issued quarterly in advance and are payable within forty-five days of receipt.

2.3 Brightline may suspend the services if an undisputed invoice is more than thirty days overdue, after giving ten days' written warning.

## 3. Term and Termination

3.1 Each Order Form has an initial term of twelve months and renews for further twelve-month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least sixty days before the end of the current term.

3.2 Either party may terminate this agreement with ninety days' written notice.

3.3 Either party may terminate immediately if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty days of receiving notice of it.

## 4. Data

4.1 Brightline will delete Customer's data within sixty days after termination, except copies it must keep by law.

4.2 Brightline stores Customer's data in data centers in the United States.

## 5. Liability

5.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by Customer in the six months before the event that gave rise to the claim.

## 6. General

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

6.2 Notices must be in writing and sent by email to legal@brightline.example and contracts@contoso-pharmacy.example.

Signed for Brightline Analytics, Inc. by Dana Whitfield, Chief Executive Officer, and for Contoso Pharmacy, LLC by Amara Lewis, General Counsel, on September 15, 2025.
