# Settlement Desk Procedure

Firm: Brightline Securities LLC
Owner: Head of Operations

## 1. Purpose

This procedure says which desk books each kind of trade, and where each desk takes its settlement deadline from.

## 2. Who books what

2.1 The Equities Desk books trades in listed stocks and exchange-traded funds.

2.2 The Alternatives Desk books trades in limited partnership interests that are not listed on an exchange.

2.3 The Syndicate Desk books new issues that the firm sells in firm commitment underwritten offerings, including offerings priced after the market closes.

## 3. Settlement deadlines

3.1 Every desk settles on the settlement cycle set by SEC Rule 15c6-1 (17 CFR 240.15c6-1), including the rule's exceptions.

3.2 When the rule changes, Compliance updates the copy of the rule in this library on the day the change takes effect, and the desks follow the updated text from then on.

3.3 A desk that agrees a different settlement date with a counterparty must record that agreement on the trade ticket at the time of the trade.
