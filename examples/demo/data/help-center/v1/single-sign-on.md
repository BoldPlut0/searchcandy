# Single sign-on (SSO)

Single sign-on with SAML, for providers such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace, is available on the Enterprise plan only.

On the Team plan, people can sign in with their Google or Microsoft accounts, but you cannot enforce sign-in through your own identity provider.

To turn on SAML single sign-on, an Enterprise account owner goes to Settings, then Security, then Single sign-on, and follows the steps for their provider.
