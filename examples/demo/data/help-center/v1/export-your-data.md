# Export your data

Account owners can export all workspace data at any time from Settings, then Data, then Export. The export is a ZIP file of CSV and JSON files, and the download link stays valid for 7 days.

After you cancel, we keep your data for 30 days so you can still export it. After 30 days we delete it permanently.
