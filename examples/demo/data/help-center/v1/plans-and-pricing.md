# Plans and pricing

Brightline has three plans.

## Starter

For small teams getting started. $29 per user per month, billed monthly. Up to 10 users. Email support.

## Team

For growing teams. $240 per user per year, billed annually at the start of each billing year. Up to 200 users. Email and live chat support. Sign in with Google or Microsoft accounts.

## Enterprise

For larger organizations. Annual contract, invoiced. Unlimited users, single sign-on (SAML), a named support manager and a phone line for urgent issues.

You can move to a bigger plan at any time; we charge the difference for the rest of your billing period. Moving to a smaller plan takes effect at your next renewal.
