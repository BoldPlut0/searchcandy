# Cancel your plan

Only the account owner can cancel. Go to Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan.

Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged again. Cancelling does not refund the current period; see the Refunds article for when a refund applies.

You can export your data before or after you cancel; see Export your data.
