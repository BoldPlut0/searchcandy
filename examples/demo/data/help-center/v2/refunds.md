# Refunds

Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of payment.

Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the start of the billing year. After that, you keep access until the end of the year and we do not refund the rest.

To ask for a refund, email billing@brightline.example from the account owner's address, with your invoice number. Refunds go back to the card or bank account that paid, within 10 business days.
